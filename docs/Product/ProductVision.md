# My Library Manager Product Vision

## Problem

Readers who own large book collections often struggle to keep track of the books and editions they own, where their books are located, and which books they have lent to others. This can lead to duplicate purchases, difficulty finding books, and forgotten loans.

## Solution

My Library Manager is a personal library management application that provides readers with a simple digital representation of their physical book collections.

The application makes cataloguing large collections quick and effortless through ISBN and bulk scanning, automatically retrieves book metadata, detects books and editions already owned, and allows users to easily search and organize their collections.

Users can also track loans and optionally share their libraries with family members, making it easier for households with large book collections to know what they own and where their books are.
